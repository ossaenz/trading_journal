⚠ What is a Wash Sale?

A wash sale occurs when you sell a security at a loss and buy the same (or substantially identical) security within 30 days before or after the sale. The IRS disallows the loss — you cannot deduct it for tax purposes. The disallowed loss is added to the cost basis of the new position.

TickerOS detects wash sales by scanning your option trade history for round-trips where you closed at a loss and then re-entered the same underlying symbol within the 30-day window. This is an estimate — always confirm with your broker's Gain/Loss statement.
